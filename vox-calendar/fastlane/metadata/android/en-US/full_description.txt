VoxCalendar is a standalone, encrypted on-device calendar that combines professional organization with the simplicity of natural language.

Key Features:
- Natural Language Scheduling: Create complex events and tasks just by speaking (e.g., "dentist in a week"). Dates and times are resolved locally via AI.
- Layered Organization: Use colored, named layers (e.g., "Personal", "Work", "Project X") instead of a rigid category tree. Each layer is independently toggleable.
- Cross-App Day Linking: A unique day-summary sheet that shows your VoxNotes and VoxExpenses alongside your calendar events for a complete view of your day.
- Standard Interoperability: Import and export ICS files for compatibility with Google Calendar, Thunderbird, and Apple Calendar.
- Multiple Views: Optimized layouts for Year, Month, Week, and Day views with a smooth, responsive interface.
- Attachment Support: Attach photos, tickets, or confirmations to any event, stored securely on your device.
- Secure P2P Sync: Sync your schedule across two phones using NFC and Bluetooth. No cloud login required.
- SQLCipher Protected: Your schedule is your business. All data is encrypted with high-grade security.
