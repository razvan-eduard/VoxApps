VoxCalendar is a standalone, encrypted on-device calendar that combines professional organization with the simplicity of natural language.

Key Features:
- Natural Language Scheduling: Create complex events and tasks just by speaking (e.g., "dentist in a week"). Dates and times are resolved locally via AI.
- Reworked To-Do Lists: Give a task a due date and it shows up on the calendar and home screen widget; star important tasks, see a "now" line and "Up next" label, and a Done toggle. Add items by voice or by scanning a photo. Tasks are edited inline on their own node, weekly recurrence picks exactly which days, and a list can be a routine that resets at midnight.
- Layered Organization: Use colored, named layers (e.g., "Personal", "Work", "Project X") instead of a rigid category tree. Each layer is independently toggleable.
- Cross-App Day Linking: A unique day-summary sheet that shows your VoxNotes and VoxExpenses alongside your calendar events for a complete view of your day.
- Standard Interoperability: Import and export ICS files for compatibility with Google Calendar, Thunderbird, and Apple Calendar.
- Multiple Views: Optimized layouts for Year, Month, Week, and Day views with a smooth, responsive interface. Day view opens scrolled to the current time, and tells you when there's "Nothing else today" once you're done.
- Attachment Support: Attach photos, tickets, or confirmations to any event via camera or gallery, stored securely on your device, with a paperclip indicator on any entry that has one.
- Secure P2P Sync: Sync your schedule across two phones using NFC and Bluetooth. No cloud login required.
- Backup & Restore: Save and restore your events, to-do lists, and settings to a file you choose, with Full override, Merge, or Additive restore modes.
- SQLCipher Protected: Your schedule is your business. All data is encrypted with high-grade security.
- Finished Items Read as Finished: A done to-do is flat grey behind a black outline, not a faded copy of itself — so a list of completed items doesn't read as a list of dimmed ones.
- Months Land Somewhere Sensible: Switching month selects the first of that month; coming back to this month selects today, instead of keeping a day-of-month you never chose.
