VoxCommander is an on-device voice assistant for Android. It handles wake word detection, speech-to-text (STT), and natural language understanding (NLU) all locally on your device.

Features:
- Wake Word Detection: Always-on listening with Vosk, Porcupine, or OpenWakeWord.
- Speech-to-Text: On-device Whisper.cpp with multilingual support.
- Natural Language Understanding: Triple AI Brain (Regex -> Primary LLM -> Offline fallback).
- Intent Routing: Maps commands to system actions or companion Vox apps.
- Privacy-focused: All processing happens on-device.

VoxCommander is the heart of the VoxApps ecosystem, a collection of independent apps that can be controlled by voice.
