VoxExpenses is a standalone, encrypted on-device financial tracker that puts you in full control of your spending data. It offers multiple automated capture paths while maintaining absolute privacy.

Key Features:
- Triple Capture Path: Log expenses via natural language voice commands (VoxCommander), high-precision receipt scanning (VoxVision), or automatic capture from bank notifications.
- Durable Notification Queue: An opt-in listener service that inspects payment notifications. Requests are durably queued and retried to ensure no transaction is missed, even if the app is stopped.
- Rule-Based Duplicate Engine: A powerful, user-configurable engine modeled on email filters. Detect and merge duplicates based on amount, vendor, bank, or time windows with fuzzy matching support.
- Data-Quality Scoring: Intelligent merge logic that prioritizes the most trustworthy source (Manual > Scan > Notification > Voice) when combining records.
- Merchant Category Memory: Learns from your manual corrections to auto-apply categories to future transactions from the same vendor.
- Multi-Currency Reports: Visual breakdowns and spending alerts with support for home-currency conversion and custom decimal separators.
- P2P Sync: Secure, cloud-free bidirectional sync between two devices using NFC pairing and Bluetooth transfer.
- SQLCipher Security: Your entire financial history is encrypted at rest.
