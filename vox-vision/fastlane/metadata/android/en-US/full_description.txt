VoxVision turns your smartphone into a privacy-first, high-precision document scanner. It operates entirely on-device, ensuring your sensitive documents never leave your hands.

Key Features:
- Smart Auto-Capture: Intelligent document edge detection using Otsu-threshold brightness detection. The camera automatically triggers a capture once the document is stable and aligned.
- OpenCV Precision: Professional-grade edge cropping and perspective correction powered by a custom-built OpenCV engine.
- Local OCR: High-speed text recognition via an on-device PaddleOCR SDK. No network round-trips required.
- Ecosystem Integration: Forwards cleaned and titled text directly to VoxNotes as a new record or to VoxExpenses as a financial entry.
- Multimodal AI: Support for sending high-detail photos to compatible AI engines for advanced document analysis.
- Total Privacy: Zero cloud dependencies. All image processing and text extraction happen locally on your hardware.
- Multi-language UI: Available in English, Romanian, German, and French.
