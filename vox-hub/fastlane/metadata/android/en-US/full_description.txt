VoxHub is the central coordination utility for the VoxApps ecosystem, focusing on backup, restore, and private data synchronization. It maintains a "zero-knowledge" profile, holding no local database for your records.

Key Features:
- Zero-Config App Discovery: Automatically identifies every installed Vox app (Notes, Expenses, Calendar, Vision) and provides a unified interface for data management.
- Unified Encrypted Backups: Create dated ZIP bundles containing settings, data, and attachments from all selected apps. Supports scheduled backups with configurable retention.
- Selective Import: Preview record counts from backup files and choose exactly which apps and data types to restore.
- NFC/Bluetooth P2P Sync: The heart of the cloud-free experience. Pair two phones with a physical tap (NFC) to exchange identity and AES-256 keys, then sync your data bidirectionally via Bluetooth.
- App Wake-up: Intelligent IPC handling that wakes up "stopped" companion apps to ensure they respond to backup and sync requests.
- Privacy First: Operates without a central cloud. Your data moves directly between your devices or to your chosen local storage.
- Lightweight: A minimal utility that stays out of the way until you need to secure your data.
