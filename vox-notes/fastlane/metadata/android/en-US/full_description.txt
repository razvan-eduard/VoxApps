VoxNotes is a standalone, encrypted on-device notes app. Built with security in mind using SQLCipher, your notes stay private and protected.

Features:
- Encrypted storage: Uses Room with SQLCipher and Keystore-backed passphrases.
- Categories: Organize notes with customizable categories and a coverflow picker.
- Voice integration: Create and read notes via VoxCommander voice commands.
- Smart cleanup: Uses LLM-backed hooks to merge categories and detect duplicate notes.
- Calendar view: Optional month-paged agenda view for your notes.
- Multi-language support.

Part of the VoxApps ecosystem.
