VoxNotes is a standalone, encrypted on-device notes app built for privacy and speed. It is part of the VoxApps ecosystem and can be controlled entirely by voice through VoxCommander.

Key Features:
- SQLCipher Encryption: Your notes are encrypted at rest using industry-standard SQLCipher and protected by biometric authentication.
- Smart Organization: Categories with a coverflow-style picker. The built-in AI cleanup hook helps you merge similar categories (e.g., "Shopping" and "Groceries") and find duplicate notes.
- Scan-to-Note: Seamless integration with VoxVision allows you to scan physical documents, which are then titled and categorized by AI.
- Calendar View: An optional month-paged agenda view provides a chronological perspective of your thoughts and records.
- Rich Attachments: Support for multi-photo attachments with a zoomable thumbnail strip.
- Cloud-Free Sync: Genuinely bidirectional peer-to-peer sync with another phone over NFC and Bluetooth. No cloud, no tracking, just your data on your devices.
- Multi-language UI: Available in English, Romanian, German, and French.
